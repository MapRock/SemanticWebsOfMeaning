# Appendix A: Beyond Ontology
<i>This appendix accompanies [***Semantic Webs of Meaning: Building Contextual Knowledge Graphs for Deduction and Integration***](https://technicspub.com/semantic-webs-of-meaning/) by Eugene Asahara, published by [Technics Publications](https://technicspub.com).</i>

Knowledge graphs are strong at representing entities, relationships, classifications, and explicit meaning. But many enterprise problems also involve processes that unfold over time, changing observations, uncertain evidence, decisions, actions, and feedback.

One way to extend a knowledge graph in that direction is to represent decision structures such as the OODA Loop—Observe, Orient, Decide, and Act—within or alongside the graph. These structures can connect observations to context, prior states, possible actions, outcomes, and subsequent observations.

This appendix points to several structures I have explored for extending knowledge graphs beyond ontology alone, including OODA models, strategy maps, probabilistic models, event-recognition structures, and the Insight Space Graph.

My blog, [Beyond Ontologies: OODA Loop Knowledge Graph Structures](https://eugeneasahara.com/2025/03/14/beyond-ontologies-ooda-knowledge-graph-structures/), describes these topics:

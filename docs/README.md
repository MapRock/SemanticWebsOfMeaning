
# Extended Topics

This folder contains material that extends the ideas in *Semantic Webs of Meaning: Building Contextual Knowledge Graphs for Deduction and Integration* beyond what could reasonably fit in the book.

The emphasis here is conceptual rather than administrative. These documents explore additional structures, use cases, architectural patterns, and questions that grow naturally from the book but were either outside its scope or have developed further since the manuscript was completed.

Topics may include:

* ways to extend knowledge graphs beyond conventional ontology modeling;
* relationships among knowledge graphs, LLMs, machine learning, BI, and event-based systems;
* richer forms of reasoning, strategy, process, and decision support;
* methods for capturing and scaling explicit human knowledge;
* emerging standards and approaches that affect how knowledge can be represented;
* and new ideas that arise as the surrounding AI landscape evolves.

The book provides the foundation. The material here is intended to push outward from that foundation—to explore what comes next, what does not fit neatly within traditional knowledge-graph boundaries, and how these ideas may develop as enterprise intelligence becomes more capable.

Installation instructions, configuration notes, and other operational material belong elsewhere in the repository. This folder is for the ideas.
